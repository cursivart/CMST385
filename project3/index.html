Biography

Dave Matthews Band formed in Charlottesville, Virginia, in 1991 and is led by singer-guitarist Dave Matthews, a South Africa native. The band is known for its blend of rock, jazz, folk, and funk, with a heavy emphasis on improvisational live shows, and rose to mainstream success with their 1994 major-label debut, Under the Table and Dreaming. Key members over the years have included Carter Beauford, Stefan Lessard, Boyd Tinsley, and LeRoi Moore.

Formation and early years

Dave Matthews, born in Johannesburg, South Africa, moved to Charlottesville, Virginia, in 1986. He began working as a bartender at Miller's and became part of the local music scene, which included musicians who would go on to form the band, such as Carter Beauford (drums) and LeRoi Moore (saxophone). The band officially formed in 1991, and their first public performances are often cited as the Earth Day Festival or a March 1991 show at a Charlottesville nightclub. Their 1993 independent release, Remember Two Things, became a surprise success, selling thousands of copies.

Mainstream success

After the success of Remember Two Things, the band signed with RCA Records and released their major-label debut in 1994, Under the Table and Dreaming. This album featured hit songs like "What Would You Say" and "Ants Marching" and helped to establish the band's fusion of rock with other genres. Their 1996 follow-up, Crash, further cemented their place in the music world with the popular track "Crash Into Me".

Musical style and legacy

The band's music is a blend of rock, jazz fusion, folk, and funk, characterized by intricate rhythms and improvisational performances. They are known for their energetic and dynamic live shows, which helped them build a massive and loyal fan base. The band's lineup has evolved over time, but their status as one of the most successful touring acts in modern rock history remains intact.

The Dave Matthews Band was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2024.
